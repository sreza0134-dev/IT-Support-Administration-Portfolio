# Scenario 5: Laptop won't turn on

Level: Basic
Note: Practice scenario. The details are fictional.

## Problem
A user's laptop doesn't start. The screen stays black when the power button is pressed.

## Symptoms
- The screen stays black
- No sound from the fan
- No lights on the laptop, or only the charging light

## Diagnostic steps
1. Check the power. Is the charger plugged in at the wall and the laptop? Does the charging light come on?
2. Try a different wall socket and, if one is available, a different charger of the same type.
3. Remove the charger and any USB devices. Hold the power button for 30 seconds, then reconnect the charger and try again. This clears leftover charge.
4. Check if it is only the screen. Plug in an external monitor. If an image appears, the laptop is running and the laptop screen is the problem.
5. Listen for the fan and look for any lights, which show that the laptop is trying to start.

## Root cause
The charger cable was damaged near the plug, so the battery had drained completely.

## Solution
1. Replace the charger with a working one of the same type
2. Let the laptop charge for 15 to 30 minutes, then turn it on
3. Check that it starts normally and the battery is charging
4. Ask the user to save their work and confirm the laptop works

## Prevention
- Keep a few spare chargers for common laptop models
- Tell users not to wrap the cable tightly around the charger

## What I learned
Start with power and rule out the simple cause before opening the laptop.
